# Safety and vulnerability reporting

[English](SECURITY.md) | [한국어](SECURITY-ko.md)

This toolkit is for trusted users researching offline copies they hold. It does
not connect to a real PLC or automatically launch engineering software.
Input files do not guarantee a safe code-execution environment. Inspect projects
of uncertain provenance in an isolated environment without credentials or
business data.

When reporting a vulnerability, do not post customer originals, passwords, paths,
equipment addresses, or screenshots in a public Issue. At publication time, check
whether GitHub private vulnerability reporting is enabled and use it if available.
Otherwise, first request a contact channel without sharing sensitive reproduction
materials.

Outputs are not approved programs for operating equipment. Always preserve
originals and validate separate copies. Online writes, downloads, RUN/STOP, and
field deployment are outside this project's scope.
